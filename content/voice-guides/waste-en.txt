Farm waste guidance. First identify the material. Suitable, clean crop residues may be used as mulch or compost when appropriate for the crop and soil. Do not assume every residue is safe for animals. Ask a veterinarian or agricultural expert before using it as feed. Keep plastics, pesticide containers, chemical packaging and contaminated materials separate. Never burn crop residues in the open. If the material cannot be reused or safely processed, contact your Gram Panchayat or municipal office and ask for the authorised collection or disposal route. Keep the material dry and separate while waiting for collection. Do not dump waste in drains, water bodies or public roads.
