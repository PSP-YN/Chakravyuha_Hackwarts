Welcome to Krishi Connect 360. Choose English or Kannada from the language control. Sign in using the secure email link and select your participant role in Profile. KrishiMarket connects farmers and buyers. Mandi prices are reference prices, not guaranteed offers. A buyer offer is a proposal, not payment. Review price, accepted quantity and all costs before agreeing. KrishiShare connects farmers and machinery owners. Check operation, horsepower, implement, operator, fuel, minimum duration and availability. Providers can reschedule or cancel bookings and upload before and after evidence. FarmLoop connects crop by-products and processors. Check material type, moisture, contamination, quantity and collection terms. Processor acceptance is separate from independent quality verification. KrishiTransit lets transporters publish vehicle type, load capacity, pickup, destination and availability. Agree loading, route permits, insurance and delivery terms before accepting. FPOs and cooperatives can coordinate member lots and offers. Municipality users can consult the public directory. Only administrators can publish official contacts after source verification. Reuse only suitable uncontaminated residues as mulch or compost. Confirm animal-feed safety with an expert. Keep plastic and chemical containers separate. Never burn crop residues. Contact the local Panchayat or municipality about non-reusable waste. Use Reports for listing or participant concerns and Disputes for transaction problems. The activity history records application data changes. Authentication session logs are managed separately. Expected farmer net is offer price multiplied by accepted quantity minus entered costs. It is not proof of settlement.
