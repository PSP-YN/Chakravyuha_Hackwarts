Buyer and trader guide. Sign in and choose Buyer or Trader in Profile. Browse active produce listings. Check location, quantity, unit, quality and asking price. Compare with mandi reference data, but do not treat it as a guaranteed transaction price. Submit an offer with price and quantity. Enter transport, handling, commission and other costs honestly. An offer is not payment or settlement. Wait for the seller's decision and coordinate pickup, quality inspection and delivery. Open a dispute if a transaction has a problem.
