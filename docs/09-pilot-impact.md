# Karnataka Pilot and Impact Measurement

## Hypothesis
A concentrated local pilot can test whether shared listings and structured offers improve discovery and economic transparency. A Tumakuru-area cluster is a proposed hypothesis, not a confirmed partner location. Validate crop mix, market access, machinery providers, processors and travel radius first.

## Proposed recruitment targets (not existing partners)
Proposed recruitment targets, not existing partners: 20 farmers/suppliers; 5 produce buyers; 3 machinery owners; 2 waste processors; 2 transporters; 1 FPO/cooperative; 1 district/local-body contact; and 2–3 Storage Guardian hardware test nodes. Confirm real recruitment and permissions before public claims.

## Field questions
Which crops/grades are traded? Which units and weights are used? Who pays loading/transport/commission? How is quality measured? Which machinery operations are hard to source? What is the full quote including fuel/operator/mobilisation? Which residues already serve as fodder or soil amendment? Which processors publish quality specs? What connectivity and language constraints exist?

## Metrics
- Net realisation = gross accepted sale value − farmer-borne costs.
- Additional net income = platform outcome − comparable baseline.
- Machinery savings = comparable individual service cost − group cost.
- By-product net earnings = confirmed proceeds − collection/selling costs.
- Fulfilment = completed / accepted transactions.
- Offer conversion = accepted / valid offers.
- Time to first offer = publish to first qualified offer.
- Machinery utilisation = booked serviceable hours / available serviceable hours.
- Pool acceptance = accepted pooled quantity / proposed pooled quantity.
- Transport fulfilment = jobs confirmed received / jobs accepted.
- Municipality response time = time from enquiry submission to first official response.
- Storage telemetry coverage = expected readings received / expected readings sent; do not treat this as grain-quality improvement.

## Attribution
Compare same commodity, grade, location, quantity, season and time window. Mark costs actual/quoted/estimated. Do not treat gross transaction value as income. Do not claim causality from simple before/after. Keep platform revenue, farmer proceeds and savings separate. No emissions claims without defensible counterfactual methodology.

## Pilot stages
Discovery interviews → closed beta → supervised real transactions → review quality/fulfilment/disputes/repeat use → expand only when operational reliability and unit economics justify it.
