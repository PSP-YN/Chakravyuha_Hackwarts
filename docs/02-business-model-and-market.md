# Business Model and Market Positioning

## Positioning
A transaction-coordination and farmer-economics layer across produce, machinery services and usable agricultural by-products. It complements public and private systems.

## Competitive context
- e-NAM already provides electronic market integration, lot creation, assaying, bidding, price discovery and digital payment capabilities in participating mandis. Do not claim this is the first digital agricultural marketplace.
- FARMS already connects farmers with Custom Hiring Service Centres. Its published disclaimer indicates service pricing and terms are negotiated offline. The hypothesis to test is whether structured, comparable, end-to-end operation quotes improve the workflow.
- AGMARKNET and data.gov.in provide reference datasets. A reference price is not a buyer offer.
- Local traders, WhatsApp groups, FPOs, custom-hiring centres and processors are competitors and potential partners.

Do not claim uniqueness without a dated direct feature audit.

## Value propositions
**Farmer:** compare offers by expected net proceeds; discover nearby services; find buyers for usable by-products; retain transaction history.  
**Buyer/processor:** discover structured supply; filter material/quality/quantity/location; submit traceable offers.  
**Provider:** publish serviceable capacity and terms; receive qualified enquiries.  
**FPO:** member-attributed aggregate lots, member status and collective offer follow-up.
**Transporter:** structured vehicle, capacity, route and pickup requests with delivery evidence.
**Municipality:** district-scoped waste-service enquiries and official contact visibility.
**Storage Guardian:** optional storage-condition monitoring; advisory only, not a certified grain-quality service.

## Revenue hypotheses
Potential paths after pilot validation: disclosed facilitation fee on completed value; provider subscription; FPO workspace; buyer sourcing tools; logistics coordination fee; optional hardware bundle/service. No payment collection or monetisation is active in the current pilot. Keep essential farmer workflows free during early validation. Do not pay-to-rank listings.

### Illustrative monthly gross revenue (scenario only)
| Activity | Arithmetic | Revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| Produce | 100 × ₹10,000 × 1% | ₹10,000 |
| Machinery | 80 × ₹1,500 × 5% | ₹6,000 |
| By-products | 40 × ₹5,000 × 2% | ₹4,000 |
| Total | Not a forecast or profit | ₹20,000 |

Excludes acquisition, support, refunds, tax, payment fees, field operations and infrastructure. Validate willingness to pay before charging.

## Metrics
Listing-to-qualified-enquiry, offer acceptance, accepted-to-completed fulfilment, time to first offer, expected vs reported actual net proceeds, machinery total-cost comparison, pool rejection reasons, repeat usage, support hours and contribution margin.

Do not treat gross transaction value as farmer income uplift. Keep platform revenue, farmer proceeds and farmer savings separate.

## Statistics discipline
Agriculture GVA share, holding-size data, post-harvest loss, farmer share of consumer rupee and crop-residue estimates vary by reference year, commodity and methodology. Cite publication date, reference period, unit and caveat. Historical statistics must be called historical, not 2026 current counts.
