# Startup Operating Plan

## Mission
Reduce agricultural coordination friction while improving transparency of farmer-side economics.

## Initial segments
Farmers and producer groups; local produce buyers; custom-hiring centres; machinery service providers; by-product processors; transporters; verified municipal contacts; FPOs/cooperatives. Storage Guardian is an optional hardware add-on, not a prerequisite to use the platform.

## Go-to-market
Start in one compact cluster. Recruit both supply and demand before launch. Use FPOs, local agri-input/service networks and field demonstrations. Provide Kannada and English onboarding with assisted support. Keep essential farmer workflows free during early validation.

## Operating roles
| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| CEO/founder | Strategy, partnerships, legal and commercial |
| CTO/engineering | Architecture, security, delivery and reliability |
| Product | Research, workflows, prioritisation and adoption |
| Field operations | Onboarding, listing quality, disputes and training |
| Data/impact | Provenance, metrics, baselines and reporting |

## Policies before pilot
Terms, privacy, listing quality, prohibited content, offer/cancellation/fulfilment terms, dispute escalation, retention/deletion, partner onboarding, incident response and grievance channel.

## Unit economics
Cost per activated farmer; cost per qualified buyer/provider; support cost per completed transaction; contribution margin; repeat rate; seasonality; working-capital exposure.

## Fundraising narrative
The story is a farmer-side coordination layer measuring outcomes across produce sale, machinery access, by-product recovery and transport, with FPO attribution and local-body coordination. Storage Guardian is an optional advisory sensor add-on. Evidence must be completed participant workflows and repeat behaviour; accepted offers are not proof of payment. The current pilot has no payment processing or AI-generated decisions.
